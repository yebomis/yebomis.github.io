CV@GS

My research asks how AI can contribute without taking over—when it should step forward, what it should leave open, and when it should step back. I pursue this question through three connected modes: Making, Growing, and Caring.

01 · Making / Creative AI

I design proactive and creative AI that opens possibilities without occupying the conclusion. The goal is epistemic agency: helping people surface tacit knowledge, form their own questions and criteria, and select, revise, or redirect what AI contributes.

02 · Growing / Cooperative AI

I study how cooperative AI can support each person’s growth in context—across life stages, personal circumstances, and specialized professional practice. Rather than treating age, expertise, or a profile as a fixed proxy, I explore metacognitive support that helps people examine uncertainty, express judgment, and adjust the form and timing of help.

03 · Caring / Responsible AI

I examine how AI’s perceived mind, authority, and relationships shape what people accept, question, or delegate. Responsible AI should know when to step back, support transitions and endings, and preserve people’s ability to review, redirect, and continue—without turning personalization into wider inference or control.
